# V2.2.0

## Pressure trend redesign

- Redesigned the 24-hour pressure chart to match the original issue mock-up.
- Added a labelled pressure scale with rounded 5-unit boundaries and a minimum
  10-unit range, preventing small changes from looking artificially dramatic.
- Shows the accumulated period and current pressure in the top row.
- Shows `-24h`, `-12h`, and `now` timeline labels when the history is complete.
- Shows pressure delta, minimum and maximum values in the footer.
